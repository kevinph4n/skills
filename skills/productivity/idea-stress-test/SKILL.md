# Goal
Find where the idea breaks as it grows, before the market does.

# Steps
1. Pre-mortem: it is dead in 18 months. Top 5 causes, ranked by likelihood x impact.
2. Scale: bottom-up market size, next adjacent niche, cost of user #10,000 vs #10, what breaks at 10x and 100x.
3. Money: who pays, which revenue model, rough cost to acquire a customer vs revenue per customer.
4. Distribution: how do the first 100 users find it? The next 1,000? Any free, repeatable channel?
5. Moat and risk: why can't a big player copy it fast? Platform dependency, legal or data risk, can the founder sustain 2-3 years?
6. Steelman both sides: build vs don't build.

# Rules
- Label everything FACT (sourced), ESTIMATE, or GUESS.
- Every weakness: fixable? At what cost? The user decides.

# Output
Top 3 risks with mitigations, what breaks when, unit economics, channels, 3 assumptions to test next.
If inside a project, append to `docs/idea.md`.
