---
name: idea-validate
description: Check if a concrete idea is worth building — real demand, competitors, who already failed. Use when the user has a specific idea or problem statement. Not for brainstorming.
---

# Goal
Try to kill the idea with facts. Only survivors get built.

# Steps
1. Compress the idea into one sentence: who uses it, when, and what it replaces. If that's impossible, clarify first.
2. Demand: any evidence of searches, complaints, or payment? What do people use today? "Nothing" may mean it doesn't hurt enough. Give 5 Mom Test questions (past behavior, never "would you use it?").
3. Competitors (must search): direct + indirect (including spreadsheets or doing nothing). Read their bad reviews.
4. Graveyard: who tried and died, why, and does that reason still apply?
5. Gap: which difference do users actually care about?
6. Smallest test (≤ 1 week, ~0 cost) with kill criteria set upfront.

# Rules
- Every market claim needs a source, otherwise label it "unverified".
- "No competitors" usually means you didn't look hard enough, or there's no market.

# Output
Verdict 🟢 / 🟡 / 🔴 · competitor table · how it could die · test + kill criteria · sources
