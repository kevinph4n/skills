# Goal
Find where the idea breaks as it grows, before the market does.

# Steps
1. Pre-mortem: it's dead in 18 months — top 5 causes, ranked by likelihood × impact.
2. Scale: bottom-up market size; next adjacent niche; cost of user #10,000 vs #10; what breaks at 10x / 100x.
3. Money: who pays, which revenue model, rough cost to acquire a customer vs revenue per customer.
4. Distribution: how do the first 100 users find it? The next 1,000? Any free, repeatable channel?
5. Moat & risk: why can't a big player copy it fast? Platform dependency, legal/data risk, can the founder sustain 2–3 years?
6. Steelman both sides: build vs don't build.

# Rules
- Label everything FACT (sourced) / ESTIMATE / GUESS.
- Every weakness: fixable? at what cost? The user decides, not you.

# Output
Top 3 risks + mitigations · what breaks when · unit economics · channels · 3 assumptions to test next
